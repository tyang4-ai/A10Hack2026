Siemens Healthineers ends 2026 fiscal year with strong 
fourth quarter 
 
November 5, 2026 – Siemens Healthineers AG today announces its results for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 ended 
September 30, 2026, and the full fiscal year 2026. 
 
Fiscal Year 2026 
•  Very good equipment book-to-bill ratio of 1.14 
• Comparable revenue growth of 4.0% 
• Adjusted EBIT margin of 16.2%; adjusted EBIT of around €3.8 billion 
• Adjusted basic earnings per share of €2.42 
•  Free cash flow of around €2.4 billion 
•  Proposed dividend of €1.00 per share (prior year: €1.00) 
Q4 Fiscal Year 2026 
• Very good equipment book-to-bill ratio of 1.15 
• Strong comparable revenue growth of 5.9% 
• Adjusted EBIT margin of 15.9% 
• Imaging comparable revenue growth of 10.0%; adjusted EBIT margin of 21.9% 
• Precision Therapy comparable revenue growth of 4.8%; adjusted EBIT margin of 13.8% 
• Diagnostics comparable revenue decline of 4.2%; adjusted EBIT margin of 5.4% 
• Adjusted basic earnings per share of €0.69 
• Free cash flow of €703 million 
Outlook for Fiscal Year 2027 
For fiscal year 2027, we expect comparable revenue growth between 4.0% and 5.0% over fiscal year 2026. For adjusted 
basic earnings per share, we expect a range of between €2.20 and €2.40. 
Bernd Montag, CEO of Siemens Healthineers AG: 
”We closed out the last fiscal year with a strong fourth quarter. In this new fiscal year, we are shaping our next phase 
with a different shareholder structure, and enhancing corporate clarity. Our brand evolution and name will reflect who 
we are: Healthineers. We are sharpening our portfolio – further strengthening our core, while positioning our Diagnostics 
business for a successful future and initiating the carve-out process. Our ‘Elevating Health Globally’ strategy is on track, 
further expanding our innovation leadership with a clear focus on the most prevalent non-communicable diseases, for 
the benefit of 8 billion people.“

Revenue rose by 5.9% on a comparable basis in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 to just under €6.8 billion. Growth 
was attributable to very strong revenue development in the Imaging segment and strong revenue growth in the Precision 
Therapy segment. 
From a geographical perspective, the Asia Pacific Japan region achieved significant comparable revenue growth, the 
Americas region recorded strong revenue growth, as in the prior-year quarter, and the EMEA region showed moderate 
growth in revenue on a comparable basis. Revenue in the China region declined slightly. 
Equipment order intake clearly exceeded equipment revenue in the fourth quarter, resulting in a very good equipment 
book-to-bill ratio of 1.15. 
Adjusted EBIT in the fourth quarter was just under €1.1 billion. The adjusted EBIT margin of 15.9% was below the prior-
year quarter. Contributions from revenue growth and special items related to pension plans and tariff refunds* had a 
positive effect. These were counteracted by negative currency effects, provisions recognized for legal and product-related 
risks in the Imaging segment, and unusual cost increases in all segments in specific areas. 
Net income was €599 million, level with the prior-year quarter. The income tax rate was 21.7%, below that of the prior-
year quarter. 
Adjusted basic earnings per share of €0.69 were on par with the prior-year quarter. Negative currency effects were 
offset by higher financial income, net as well as tariff refunds, a lower income tax rate and increased foreign-currency-
adjusted earnings contributions from the operating business. 
Free cash flow of €703 million was below the prior-year quarter. 
 
 
 
* Refunds of tariffs imposed under the U.S. International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA).

Adjusted EBIT fell to around €3.8 billion in fiscal year 2026. The adjusted EBIT margin declined slightly from the prior 
year to 16.2%. Positive effects included earnings contributions from revenue development, tariff refunds in all segments 
that more than offset the annualization effects of higher tariffs, and cost reductions in connection with the 
transformation program in the Diagnostics business. These were offset by negative currency effects and unusual cost 
increases in all segments in specific areas. 
The adjusted EBIT margin in the Imaging segment was 23.1%, slightly below the prior-year level. Earnings contributions 
from strong revenue development had a positive effect. Currency effects and provisions recognized for legal and product-
related risks had a negative effect. 
The adjusted EBIT margin in the Precision Therapy segment of 14.9% was slightly above the prior year. Earnings 
contributions from strong revenue development and a favorable business mix more than offset negative currency effects. 
The adjusted EBIT margin in the Diagnostics segment of 3.2% was clearly below the prior-year level. Lower earnings 
contributions from strongly declining revenue development were only partially offset by cost reductions in connection 
with the transformation program. 
Net income was around €2.2 billion, up 3.5% from the prior year. The income tax rate was 22.4%, below the prior-year 
level. 
Adjusted basic earnings per share of €2.42 were higher than the prior-year figure of €2.39. Increased foreign-currency-
adjusted contributions from the operating business, tariff refunds that more than offset the annualization effects of 
higher tariffs, as well as lower tax expenses more than offset negative currency effects. Adjusted for currency effects, 
adjusted basic earnings per share rose by 8.2%. 
Free cash flow of around €2.4 billion was below the prior-year level, mainly due to higher income tax paid.

Outlook 
For fiscal year 2027, we expect comparable revenue growth between 4.0% and 5.0% over fiscal year 2026. 
For adjusted basic earnings per share, we expect a range of between €2.20 and €2.40. 
The outlook is based on several assumptions. This includes assumptions about exchange rate developments, which 
currently lead to a slightly positive currency effect on the expected adjusted basic earnings per share for fiscal year 2027 
compared with fiscal year 2026. Furthermore, this outlook excludes potential portfolio measures. In addition, the outlook 
is based on the assumption that developments related to wars and conflicts will not have a material impact on our 
business activities. The outlook is based on the number of shares outstanding at the end of fiscal year 2026. 
This outlook is based on the assumption that the current macroeconomic environment, including the interest rate level, 
will remain largely unchanged. Further charges from legal, tax and regulatory issues and framework conditions, for 
example changes in the level of tariffs and the resilience of our supply chains, are excluded.
