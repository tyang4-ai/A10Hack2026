# SHL sources

This plan is frozen for the demo. `plan.json` is the plan itself, and `backend/scripts/check_plan.py` checks it. Every URL below was opened on Sat Nov 7, 2026, and every page index was checked against the downloaded PDF (`check_plan.py --pages <folder outside the repo>`). Page numbers are 1-based indices in the PDF file, not the printed labels. In the FY2025 report they happen to match. In the FY2019 report they don't.

Archive sources (S-011 to S-016, plan v2) were collected before the event, on Aug 1, 2026, as part of Gordon's summer 2026 archive. The run serves them from the S3 document cache, and every fetch is still decided by the gateway first. Each has its original public URL, taken from the archive's `.meta.md` sidecar, and all six still answered live on Nov 7. Their pages were checked against the archive's own PDFs, opened in place and never copied into the repo (`check_plan.py --archive <archive folder>`). Only primary documents were used. None of Gordon's notes, analysis or scripts were opened.

Base year: FY2026 (to Sep 30, 2026) from the Nov 5, 2026 results release, with full statements and notes from the FY2025 Annual Report, because the FY2026 Annual Report is only due Nov 26, 2026. History goes back to FY2017, so ROTIC has ten fiscal years (FY2017 to FY2026).

How the archive sources were picked:
- The plan already reads the income statements and balance sheets for FY2018 to FY2025 from S-001 and S-003 to S-005, because each report also gives the prior year. So the even-year reports (S-011 to S-013) add only what is missing: cash flows, tax rates, debt and net debt, and segments. Reading their statements again would give the drafter two copies of each year and push the ROTIC group toward its 400-row cap.
- The FY2018 report is read in full because it holds FY2017 and the Oct 1, 2016 balance sheet, which make the tenth year.
- Philips's annual reports add a peer read the same way as SHL. GE HealthCare's 10-Ks in the archive are HTML only and would be read whole, more than a hundred pages of text each, so GE HealthCare stays with its XBRL facts (S-007).
- Left out: the FY2015 to FY2017 combined financial statements from the IPO (pre IFRS 15, and FY2017 is already covered), the odd-year archive copies (the same files as S-001 and S-003 to S-005), quarterly reports, LSEG transcripts and slides (third-party copies), other competitors (Canon and Roche report medical technology inside larger groups, and the archive has a single United Imaging year) and every regulatory or product document. research_stage is about the analyst's own coverage history, which code always leaves as a declared gap, so no document in the archive fits its rubric.

Notes for the run:
- The FY2019 and FY2021 PDFs carry soft hyphens, BEL control characters (U+0007) and thin spaces between a minus sign and its digits (`– 8,722`). Quotes and number parsing have to cope with them.
- data.sec.gov answered every GET with the default `HTTP_USER_AGENT`, but HEAD got an occasional 403. If the run gets a 403, Gordon sets `HTTP_USER_AGENT` with a contact.
- The research_stage criterion is about the analyst's own coverage history. No source can answer that, so N-07 brings R&D and outlook evidence and the coverage part stays a declared gap.
